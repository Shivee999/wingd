The Build

Every wing goes through the same five stops before it reaches you. Nothing is pre-sauced, nothing sits under a lamp.

01

Brined

Whole-cut chicken, brined overnight so the meat stays wet after frying.

02

Dredged

Seasoned dredge for the shell that holds sauce without going soft.

03

Double fried

Fried once to cook, rested, fried again to order for the crack.

04

Sauced

Tossed wet or hit with a dry rub — eight Wing'd sauces, your pick.

05

Boxed hot

Vented boxes so steam escapes and the crust survives the ride.
